Professional Wet Room & Walk-In Shower Installation

Wet rooms and walk-in showers have become one of the most sought-after bathroom upgrades across the South East. They create a sense of openness and luxury, make smaller bathrooms feel more spacious, and offer a practical, easy-to-clean showering solution for households of all sizes.

At HGN Bathrooms, we specialise in designing and installing wet rooms and walk-in showers that are built to last. Every installation begins with professional-grade waterproof tanking to ensure the entire shower area is fully sealed, followed by precision drainage installation, high-quality tiling and a clean, contemporary finish.

Whether you are converting an existing bathroom into a wet room, replacing an old shower cubicle with a modern walk-in enclosure, or creating a new shower space as part of a full renovation, we deliver the same meticulous standard of workmanship on every project.

What Does a Wet Room Installation Include?

A properly installed wet room requires specialist preparation that goes beyond a standard shower fitting. Our wet room installations include:

  • Floor levelling and gradient creation for proper water drainage
  • Full waterproof tanking of the floor and walls using professional-grade membrane systems
  • Linear or point drainage installation with high-flow waste connections
  • Floor-to-ceiling tiling with anti-slip options for the shower zone
  • Frameless glass screens or panels (if desired)
  • Thermostatic shower valve and rainfall or handheld shower head installation
  • All plumbing connections and pipework
  • Finishing, sealing and grouting

Why Choose HGN Bathrooms?

Wet rooms require a higher level of precision than a standard bathroom fit. Incorrect waterproofing or poor drainage gradients can lead to leaks, damp and costly remedial work. We have extensive experience with wet room installations and understand exactly what is needed to create a watertight, long-lasting result.

Haydn personally manages and carries out every installation, ensuring consistent quality and clear communication throughout the project. We never subcontract the critical waterproofing or tiling work — it is all done in-house to the highest standard.

Popular Wet Room and Shower Scenarios

Many homeowners come to us wanting to replace an old bath-shower combination with a spacious walk-in shower or wet room. This is particularly popular in en-suites where space is at a premium, and in family bathrooms where a large, accessible shower is more practical than a bath.

We also work with homeowners who need level-access showering for accessibility reasons. A properly designed wet room with flush-to-floor drainage provides a safe, barrier-free shower area that is both stylish and fully functional.

Whatever your situation, we will visit your property, assess the space and provide an honest, detailed quote for the work involved.

FAQ

Wet Room & Shower FAQs

Common questions about wet room and shower installations.

What is the difference between a wet room and a walk-in shower?▾
A wet room is a fully waterproofed bathroom where the shower area is open and level with the rest of the floor, with no tray or enclosure. A walk-in shower typically has a shower tray and may include a glass panel or partial enclosure. Both are popular choices — we install either to suit your space and preference.
Can you install a wet room in any bathroom?▾
In most cases yes. Wet rooms can be installed in bathrooms of almost any size, including small en-suites. The key requirement is proper waterproof tanking and drainage. We assess your floor structure and plumbing during our free consultation to confirm suitability.
How much does a wet room installation cost?▾
A typical wet room installation ranges from £3,500 to £8,000 depending on room size, tile specification and drainage requirements. This includes tanking, drainage, tiling and all plumbing. We provide a detailed quote before any work begins.
How long does it take to install a wet room?▾
Most wet room installations take between 4 and 7 working days. This includes floor preparation, waterproof tanking, drainage installation, tiling and finishing. Complex projects with structural floor work may take slightly longer.
Will a wet room cause leaks or damp problems?▾
Not when installed correctly. We use professional-grade waterproof tanking systems that fully seal the floor and walls, along with properly graded drainage to ensure water flows away efficiently. Our installations are built to last and will not cause damp or leaks when used as intended.
