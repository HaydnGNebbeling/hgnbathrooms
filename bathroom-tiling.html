Tiling & Panelling

Professional Bathroom Tiling & Wall Panelling Services

Tiling is one of the most visible elements of any bathroom renovation. It sets the tone, defines the style and has to stand up to daily use for years to come. At HGN Bathrooms, tiling is not just a finishing touch — it is a craft we take seriously on every project.

Whether you want clean, floor-to-ceiling porcelain tiles for a modern look, a striking geometric feature wall, natural stone for a luxury finish, or contemporary wood slat panelling to add warmth and texture, we deliver precise, high-quality results that elevate the entire room.

We also install waterproof wall panels for homeowners who prefer a grout-free, low-maintenance alternative to traditional tiling. These panels come in a wide range of finishes including marble, stone and concrete effects, and are particularly popular in shower areas and wet rooms.

Our Tiling & Panelling Services

  • Floor-to-ceiling bathroom wall tiling in porcelain, ceramic, marble and natural stone
  • Floor tiling with anti-slip options and underfloor heating compatibility
  • Feature walls with geometric patterns, herringbone, chevron or contrasting tile designs
  • Shower niche tiling with mitred edges and waterproof finishing
  • Large-format tile installation with minimal grout lines
  • Wood slat panelling and timber batten feature walls
  • Waterproof wall panels in marble, stone and concrete effect finishes
  • Mosaic tiling and decorative borders

Why Tiling Quality Matters

The difference between average tiling and exceptional tiling is in the details — straight lines, consistent grout joints, properly waterproofed edges, clean mitred corners and tiles that are perfectly level across the wall. These details may seem small, but they are what separate a bathroom that looks professionally finished from one that does not.

Haydn takes immense pride in his tiling work. Every tile is carefully measured, cut and positioned with precision. We use the correct adhesives for the surface and tile type, apply waterproof grout in wet areas, and ensure all edges are sealed and finished to the highest standard.

Wood Slat Panelling

Wood slat panelling has become one of the most popular design features in modern bathrooms. These vertical or horizontal timber battens create a warm, textured accent wall that adds depth and character to any space. We typically install slat panelling behind freestanding baths, above vanity units or as a full wall feature in larger bathrooms.

All our panelling work uses properly prepared and sealed timber. We take care to ensure the battens are evenly spaced, perfectly level and securely fixed. The result is a clean, contemporary look that photographs beautifully and transforms the feel of the room.

FAQ

Tiling & Panelling FAQs

Common questions about our tiling and wall panelling services.

What types of tiling do you offer?▾
We offer floor-to-ceiling wall tiling, floor tiling, feature walls, mosaic borders, niche tiling, large-format porcelain tiles and natural stone installation. We work with all tile sizes and materials including porcelain, ceramic, marble, travertine and slate.
What is wood slat panelling and is it suitable for bathrooms?▾
Wood slat panelling consists of vertical or horizontal timber battens fixed to a wall to create a textured, contemporary feature. When properly sealed and installed with adequate ventilation, slat panelling works beautifully in bathrooms. We recommend using it on walls away from direct water contact, such as behind a freestanding bath or above a vanity unit.
How much does bathroom tiling cost?▾
Tiling costs depend on the room size, tile type and complexity of the layout. As a guide, tiling a standard bathroom typically ranges from £1,500 to £4,000 for labour, excluding tile supply. We provide an itemised quote that clearly separates labour and materials so you know exactly what you are paying for.
Can you tile over existing tiles?▾
In some cases yes, but we generally recommend removing old tiles for the best long-term result. Tiling over existing tiles can add weight to the wall and may not provide the best adhesion. We will assess your walls during our consultation and advise on the best approach for your situation.
Do you supply the tiles or should I buy my own?▾
Either option works. Many clients enjoy choosing their own tiles, while others prefer us to source everything. We can recommend suppliers and sometimes secure trade discounts on your behalf. We are experienced with tiles from all major UK retailers and specialist suppliers.
