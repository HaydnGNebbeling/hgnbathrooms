Cloakroom & En-Suite Renovation Specialists

A cloakroom or en-suite may be the smallest room in your home, but it is often the most visited. A well-designed small bathroom creates a lasting impression on guests and adds genuine value to your property. At HGN Bathrooms, we specialise in getting the most out of compact spaces.

Whether you have a tired downstairs cloakroom that needs a complete overhaul, an en-suite that feels cramped and outdated, or even an under-stairs cupboard you want to convert into a new WC, we design and build small bathrooms that are practical, stylish and make the absolute best use of every available inch.

Our cloakroom and en-suite renovations are completed quickly and with minimal disruption. Most projects take just a few days, but the transformation is dramatic — turning a forgotten corner of your home into a space you are genuinely proud to show off.

What Does a Cloakroom or En-Suite Renovation Include?

  • Full strip-out of existing fixtures, tiles and flooring
  • Space-efficient layout design to maximise usable area
  • Wall-hung toilets and basins to free up floor space
  • Compact vanity units with built-in storage
  • Floor-to-ceiling tiling or waterproof wall panels
  • Feature walls with wood slat panelling, textured tiles or bold patterns
  • All plumbing — pipework, waste connections and tap installation
  • LED mirrors, recessed niches and discreet lighting
  • Walk-in shower or compact enclosure fitting (en-suites)

Why Choose HGN Bathrooms for Small Spaces?

Small bathrooms demand a higher level of precision and planning than larger rooms. Every millimetre counts, and poor layout choices can make the difference between a room that feels spacious and one that feels cramped. We understand the unique challenges of compact spaces and bring practical solutions to every project.

Wall-hung sanitaryware is one of our go-to techniques for cloakrooms. By mounting the toilet and basin on the wall, we free up valuable floor space that makes the room feel significantly larger. Combined with large-format tiles that minimise grout lines and a well-placed mirror, even the smallest cloakroom can feel open and contemporary.

Common Cloakroom & En-Suite Scenarios

Many homeowners come to us with dated cloakrooms that have not been touched in decades. Others want to add a shower to a bedroom en-suite that currently only has a toilet and basin. We also regularly convert unused cupboards and under-stair spaces into functional new cloakrooms, adding real convenience and value to a property.

Whatever your starting point, we will visit your property, measure the space carefully, discuss your ideas and provide a clear, honest quote. No project is too small for us to take seriously.

FAQ

Cloakroom & En-Suite FAQs

Common questions about cloakroom and en-suite renovations.

How much does a cloakroom renovation cost?▾
A cloakroom renovation typically costs between £2,000 and £4,500 depending on the specification and finishes. A simple refresh with new sanitaryware and tiling sits at the lower end, while a full strip-out with feature panelling and premium fixtures will be at the higher end. We provide a detailed quote before starting.
How long does a cloakroom or en-suite renovation take?▾
Most cloakroom renovations are completed in 2 to 4 working days. An en-suite renovation typically takes 4 to 7 working days depending on complexity. We will give you a clear timeline before we start so you can plan accordingly.
Can you add a shower to my en-suite?▾
In most cases yes. We regularly add walk-in showers and compact shower enclosures to en-suites. The key factors are available space, drainage access and water pressure. We will assess your en-suite during our free consultation and recommend the best solution for your layout.
What space-saving solutions do you recommend for small bathrooms?▾
We use a range of space-saving techniques including wall-hung toilets and basins that free up floor space, compact vanity units with built-in storage, recessed shower niches instead of bulky caddies, large-format tiles that reduce grout lines and make the room feel larger, and mirrored cabinets that add storage without taking up space.
Can you convert a cupboard under the stairs into a cloakroom?▾
Yes, we have experience converting under-stair spaces into functional cloakrooms. The main requirements are access to water supply and drainage, and sufficient headroom for a toilet. We can assess your space and advise on feasibility during a free consultation.
