Full Bathroom Renovations Across Surrey, Kent & Sussex

A bathroom renovation is one of the most impactful improvements you can make to your home. Whether your bathroom is outdated, poorly laid out, or simply due for an upgrade, HGN Bathrooms delivers complete transformations that combine practical design with a premium finish.

Run by Haydn, we handle every stage of your renovation under one roof. From the initial strip-out and waste removal to plumbing, tiling, wall panelling, sanitaryware installation and final decoration, you deal with one tradesman who takes full ownership of your project from start to finish.

We work with homeowners across Surrey, Kent and Sussex, renovating bathrooms of all sizes in properties ranging from period cottages and Victorian terraces to modern new-builds and apartment conversions.

What Does a Full Bathroom Renovation Include?

Every bathroom renovation we undertake is tailored to the homeowner, but a typical full renovation includes:

  • Complete strip-out of existing bathroom including sanitaryware, tiles and flooring
  • Waste removal and responsible disposal
  • All plumbing work — pipework, waste connections, radiator repositioning and tap installations
  • Floor-to-ceiling tiling with precision cutting and waterproof grouting
  • Feature walls, wood slat panelling or waterproof wall panels if preferred
  • Installation of bath, shower, toilet, basin and all sanitaryware
  • Underfloor heating preparation and connection (if required)
  • Finishing touches — sealant, trims, mirrors, accessories

Why Choose HGN Bathrooms for Your Renovation?

Homeowners choose us because we deliver a consistently high standard of work without the typical frustrations of dealing with multiple trades. Haydn personally manages and completes every project, which means clear communication, reliable timelines and no corners cut.

We are a small, dedicated operation rather than a large franchise, which means you get genuine care and attention on every job. Our reputation has been built entirely on word-of-mouth referrals and verified five-star reviews from real customers across the region.

Whether you have a clear vision for your new bathroom or need help choosing layouts, tiles and fixtures, we work with you to create a space that suits your home, your lifestyle and your budget.

Common Renovation Scenarios

Many of our clients come to us with bathrooms that are 15 to 20 years old and ready for a complete overhaul. Others are dealing with poor previous workmanship, leaks, mould or impractical layouts. We also work with homeowners who are renovating a newly purchased property and want to put their own stamp on the space.

Whatever your situation, we will visit your property, discuss your requirements in detail, and provide an honest, itemised quote with no hidden costs. We are always happy to offer design suggestions and practical advice based on our experience.

FAQ

Bathroom Renovation FAQs

Common questions from homeowners across Surrey, Kent & Sussex.

How much does a full bathroom renovation cost? ▾
A standard full bathroom renovation typically starts from around £4,000–£6,000 for mid-range fittings and tiling. High-end renovations with luxury fixtures, underfloor heating and bespoke tiling can range from £8,000–£15,000+. We provide a detailed, itemised quote before any work begins so there are no surprises.
How long does a full bathroom renovation take? ▾
Most full bathroom renovations take between 5 and 10 working days depending on complexity. A straightforward refit with standard tiling and sanitaryware is typically quicker, while projects involving structural changes, wet rooms or bespoke tiling may take a little longer. We always provide a clear timeline before starting.
Do you handle everything or do I need other tradespeople? ▾
We handle the entire renovation from start to finish — plumbing, tiling, wall panelling, flooring and all finishing touches. You do not need to coordinate other trades. The only exception is specialist electrical work such as rewiring, for which we work with trusted local electricians.
Can I use my own tiles and fittings? ▾
Absolutely. We are happy to work with materials you have already purchased. Alternatively, we can help you source everything and often secure trade pricing on tiles, sanitaryware and fixtures from local and national suppliers.
What areas do you cover for bathroom renovations? ▾
We cover Surrey, Kent and Sussex including towns such as Guildford, Reigate, Sevenoaks, Tonbridge, East Grinstead, Crawley and surrounding villages. If you are unsure whether we cover your area, just get in touch.
