FAQ

Bathroom Renovation FAQs

Common questions from East Grinstead homeowners.

How much does a bathroom renovation cost in East Grinstead? ▾
Costs vary depending on the size of the room and spec of fittings. A typical bathroom renovation in East Grinstead starts from around £4,000–£6,000 for a standard fit with mid-range tiles and sanitaryware. A full luxury renovation with bespoke tiling and high-end fixtures will be higher. We always provide a detailed, itemised free quote before any work begins — no surprises.
How long does a bathroom renovation take? ▾
A standard full bathroom renovation typically takes 5–10 working days from strip-out to final finish, depending on complexity. Cloakroom refurbs can be done in 2–3 days. We'll give you a clear timeline before we start so you know exactly when you'll have your bathroom back.
Do you cover East Grinstead and the surrounding villages? ▾
Yes — we're based in Lingfield, just minutes from East Grinstead, so we cover the whole area including Forest Row, Crawley Down, Dormansland, Felbridge, Hartfield and Turners Hill. If you're not sure whether we cover your postcode, just give us a call or send a message.
Do you supply the bathroom fittings or do I need to? ▾
We're flexible — we can work with fittings you've already purchased, or we can help you source everything if you prefer. We have good relationships with local suppliers and can often get trade pricing on sanitaryware, tiles, and fixtures.
Are you fully insured to work in East Grinstead? ▾
Yes, HGN Bathrooms is fully insured with public liability insurance. We're happy to share details on request. We also have a strong track record on MyBuilder with verified 5-star reviews from homeowners across Sussex and Surrey.
