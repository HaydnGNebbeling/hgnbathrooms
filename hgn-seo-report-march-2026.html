Section 2

Google Analytics 4 — What It Does & Why It Matters

Google Analytics 4 (GA4) is now live on hgnbathrooms.co.uk. Think of it as a dashboard that tells you exactly what's happening on the website — where visitors come from, what they look at, and whether they get in touch.

What GA4 tracks for HGN Bathrooms

Where visitors come from

Google search, direct, social media, referral links. Shows which marketing is working and which isn't.

Which pages get the most traffic

You'll be able to see exactly which location pages (Guildford, Oxted, Redhill etc.) are bringing in visitors.

Which areas generate enquiries

Combined with the contact form, you can start to see which towns send the most leads — and focus more effort there.

How people use the site

How long they spend, which pages they visit before getting in touch, and where they drop off.

📍 Where to find the data

  • Realtime — see live visitors right now
  • Reports → Engagement → Pages and screens — see which pages get traffic
  • Reports → Acquisition → Traffic acquisition — see where visitors come from
  • Filter page paths by bathroom-renovation- to see location page performance

📅 Data timeline

  • Days 1–14: Realtime works, reports show very little
  • Weeks 3–4: First meaningful data — pages, sources, locations
  • Month 2: Enough to spot which towns are working
  • Month 3+: Solid trends — use to guide where to focus
Section 3

SEO Plan Going Forward

SEO is not a one-time job — it builds over time. The work done today gives the site a strong foundation. What follows is the ongoing plan to grow visibility and generate more local enquiries.

Every Monday

Automated SEO audit email

GitHub runs a health check on all 55 pages and emails Hope a report. Any broken pages, missing tags or errors are flagged automatically — no manual work needed.

Monthly

Review GSC performance data

Check Google Search Console for new queries, pages with high impressions but low click rates, and any new coverage issues. Use this to decide which pages to improve or which new content to write.

Ongoing

Google reviews

Every new 5-star review on the Google Business Profile directly improves ranking in the local map pack. This is the single highest-impact action Haydn can take. A review request template is included in this document (Section 5).

Quarterly

Content and location review

Look at which location pages are getting traffic and which aren't. Add new blog posts targeting questions that are getting search impressions. Expand or refresh pages that are ranking but not converting to clicks.

Annually

Full site review

Update pricing references, refresh blog post dates, check that all location pages still reflect current service areas, and review the overall site structure as the business grows.

Section 5

Google Review Request Templates

Reviews are the fastest way to improve local search ranking. Ask every happy customer — ideally the day after the job finishes while it's still fresh. Below are three versions: WhatsApp/text, email, and a verbal ask.

📱

WhatsApp / Text Message

Short, casual, easy to reply to. Best sent the day after completion.

Template — WhatsApp / SMS

Hi [Name], it was great working on your bathroom — really pleased with how it came out! If you've got a spare two minutes, a Google review would mean the world to us. Here's the link: [YOUR GOOGLE REVIEW LINK]

Totally fine if not — just let us know if you ever need anything else. Thanks again, Haydn 👍

Tip: Replace [YOUR GOOGLE REVIEW LINK] with your Google Business Profile review link. Find it in Google Business Profile → Ask for reviews → Copy link.

✉️

Email Version

For customers who prefer email. Slightly more formal but still friendly.

Template — Email

Subject: Thank you — and a small favour

Hi [Name],

Thank you so much for choosing HGN Bathrooms for your renovation. It was a pleasure working in your home and I hope you're really happy with the result.

If you have a couple of minutes, leaving us a Google review would be a huge help — it makes a real difference to a small local business like ours.

Here's the link: [YOUR GOOGLE REVIEW LINK]

No pressure at all — and don't hesitate to get in touch if you ever need anything in future.

Many thanks,
Haydn
HGN Bathrooms
07584 624 621

🗣️

Verbal Ask (on the day)

The most natural moment to ask is right after the customer sees the finished bathroom for the first time.

What to say in person

"Really glad you like it. If you get a chance, a Google review genuinely helps us out — it only takes a couple of minutes. I'll send you a link on WhatsApp now so you've got it."

Then send the WhatsApp template immediately while you're still there. The conversion rate is far higher when the link is sent on the spot.

📌 How to find your Google review link

  • Go to business.google.com
  • Select your HGN Bathrooms profile
  • Click "Ask for reviews"
  • Copy the short link and save it in your phone contacts for quick access
