Bathroom Renovations in Crowborough — What to Expect

Crowborough sits at one of the highest points in East Sussex, on the edge of Ashdown Forest. The town has a charming mix of Victorian properties in the centre and rural homes scattered through the surrounding countryside. Many of these homes have generous bathroom spaces but dated fixtures.

The elevated position of Crowborough means some properties have excellent mains water pressure while others rely on header tanks. We always assess your system before planning works and recommend the best hot water solution for your specific property.

Crowborough is about 25 minutes east of Lingfield. We regularly work in the town and across the Ashdown Forest villages including Rotherfield, Mark Cross, and Jarvis Brook.

Local Tip for Crowborough Homeowners

Crowborough's Victorian homes often have beautiful original features like high ceilings and sash windows in the bathroom. We design renovations that complement these period details rather than fighting against them.

How much does a bathroom renovation cost in Crowborough?▾
Costs vary depending on room size and spec. A typical bathroom renovation in Crowborough starts from around £4,000–£6,000 for a standard fit with mid-range tiles and sanitaryware. Luxury renovations with bespoke tiling and high-end fixtures will be higher. We always provide a detailed, itemised free quote before any work begins — no surprises.
How long does a bathroom renovation take?▾
A standard full bathroom renovation typically takes 5–10 working days from strip-out to final finish. Cloakroom refurbs can be done in 2–3 days. We'll give you a clear timeline before we start.
