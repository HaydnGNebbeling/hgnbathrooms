Utility Rooms

Professional Utility & Laundry Room Fit-Outs

A well-planned utility room takes the pressure off your kitchen and bathroom by providing a dedicated space for washing, drying, cleaning supplies and household tasks. At HGN Bathrooms, we design and build utility rooms that are practical, well-plumbed and finished to the same high standard as our bathroom work.

Whether you are fitting out a new utility room from scratch, upgrading an existing laundry area, or converting a garage or spare room into a functional space, we handle everything from plumbing and drainage to tiling, worktops and storage.

Our utility room fit-outs are particularly popular with homeowners who are also having their bathroom or kitchen renovated. By combining the plumbing work across multiple rooms, we can often offer a more efficient and cost-effective service.

What Does a Utility Room Fit-Out Include?

  • Plumbing connections for washing machines, tumble dryers and dishwashers
  • Utility sink or Belfast sink installation with hot and cold feeds
  • Waste and drainage connections for all appliances
  • Worktop installation for folding, sorting and general use
  • Floor tiling or waterproof vinyl flooring
  • Wall tiling, splashbacks or waterproof wall panels
  • Storage solutions — shelving, cabinets and under-worktop units
  • Quooker or boiling water tap installation
  • Outside tap fitting for garden access

Why Choose HGN Bathrooms?

Utility rooms might not be the most glamorous space in the house, but they need to be built properly. Poorly plumbed washing machine connections, inadequate drainage and badly routed waste pipes are some of the most common issues we see in homes across Surrey, Kent and Sussex.

We take the same care with utility room plumbing as we do with our bathroom work. Every connection is tested, waste pipes are routed at the correct gradient, and all supply lines are properly isolated so individual appliances can be shut off without affecting the rest of the house. The result is a reliable, well-functioning space that will not cause problems down the line.

Common Utility Room Scenarios

Many of our clients are upgrading from a cramped kitchen corner where the washing machine currently lives. Others are converting an unused garage, boot room or rear porch into a dedicated utility space. We also help homeowners who are building extensions and need plumbing installed in a new utility room from the ground up.

Some clients combine their utility room fit-out with a full bathroom renovation or wider plumbing work around the house. This can be a smart way to keep disruption to a minimum and benefit from a single tradesman handling all the connected plumbing.

Whatever your situation, we will visit your property, discuss your needs and provide a clear, itemised quote with no hidden costs.

FAQ

Utility Room FAQs

Common questions about utility and laundry room fit-outs.

What does a utility room fit-out include?▾
A typical utility room fit-out includes plumbing for washing machines and tumble dryers, sink installation, waste and drainage connections, tiling or waterproof flooring, wall finishes, storage solutions and worktop installation. We tailor every fit-out to your specific needs and available space.
How much does a utility room fit-out cost?▾
Costs vary depending on the scope of the work. A straightforward plumbing and connection job for appliances can start from a few hundred pounds. A full utility room fit-out with tiling, a sink, worktops and storage typically ranges from £2,500 to £6,000. We provide a detailed quote before any work begins.
Can you convert a garage or spare room into a utility room?▾
Yes, we regularly help homeowners convert garages, spare rooms and other underused spaces into functional utility rooms. The key requirements are access to water supply and drainage. We will assess your space and advise on the best approach during a free consultation.
How long does a utility room fit-out take?▾
Most utility room fit-outs are completed in 3 to 5 working days depending on the scope of work. Simple plumbing connections for appliances can be done in a single day. We will provide a clear timeline before we start.
Do you install utility sinks and Quooker taps?▾
Yes, we install utility sinks, Belfast sinks, Quooker taps and standard mixer taps. We handle all plumbing connections including hot and cold water supply and waste drainage. Quooker installations include the tap unit and the boiler tank beneath the sink.
