Bathroom Renovation FAQs

Common questions from Tunbridge Wells homeowners.

How much does a bathroom renovation cost in Tunbridge Wells?▾
Costs vary depending on room size and spec. A typical bathroom renovation in Tunbridge Wells starts from around £4,000–£6,000 for a standard fit with mid-range tiles and sanitaryware. Luxury renovations with bespoke tiling and high-end fixtures will be higher. We always provide a detailed, itemised free quote before any work begins — no surprises.
How long does a bathroom renovation take?▾
A standard full bathroom renovation typically takes 5–10 working days from strip-out to final finish. Cloakroom refurbs can be done in 2–3 days. We'll give you a clear timeline before we start.
Do you cover Tunbridge Wells and surrounding areas?▾
Yes — we cover Tunbridge Wells and all surrounding villages and towns in Kent. If you're unsure whether we cover your postcode, just give us a call or drop us a message and we'll confirm straight away.
Do you supply the bathroom fittings or do I need to?▾
We're flexible — we can work with fittings you've already purchased, or help you source everything. We have good relationships with suppliers and can often get trade pricing on sanitaryware, tiles and fixtures.
