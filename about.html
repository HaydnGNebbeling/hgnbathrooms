Who Is HGN Bathrooms?

HGN Bathrooms is run by Haydn — a professional bathroom installer based in Lingfield, Surrey, serving homeowners across Surrey, Kent and Sussex.

Haydn personally manages and carries out every project, from the initial consultation and design right through to the final clean-up. When you hire HGN Bathrooms, you're working directly with the person doing the work — no subcontractors, no miscommunication, no surprises.

What We Do

We specialise in complete bathroom renovations — full strip-outs, plumbing, tiling, wall panelling, wet rooms, cloakrooms, en-suites and utility rooms. Whether you're refreshing a tired bathroom or building something from scratch, we handle every stage of the process.

Our work includes:

  • Full bathroom renovations — strip-out, plumbing, tiling, fixtures and finishing
  • Wet room installations — waterproof tanking, linear drains, level-access showers
  • Plumbing services — Quooker taps, radiators, boiler connections, pipework
  • Tiling and wall panelling — floor-to-ceiling tiling, wood slat panels, feature walls
  • Cloakrooms and en-suites — small space design with space-saving fixtures
  • Utility room fit-outs — plumbing, drainage, cabinetry and flooring

Our Approach

Every project starts with a free, no-obligation site visit. Haydn will discuss your ideas, take measurements, and put together a detailed quote with no hidden costs. We believe in honest pricing and clear communication throughout.

During the work, we keep things clean and tidy, protect your home, and keep you informed at every stage. Most bathroom renovations are completed in 5-10 working days, and we always aim to cause as little disruption as possible.
