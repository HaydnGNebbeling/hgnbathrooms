How It Works

Simple from start to finish

No complicated forms or confusing processes. Here's exactly what happens when you get in touch.

1

Get in touch

Call, WhatsApp or drop us a message — whatever's easiest. Tell us a little about your bathroom and what you're thinking. Hope will come back to you quickly, usually the same day.

2

We'll give you a call

Before anything else, Hope will have a quick chat to understand what you're after, your rough timescales and whether we're the right fit. No hard sell — just a friendly conversation.

3

Haydn pops round to take a look

We'll book a time that suits you for Haydn to visit, have a look at the space, talk through your ideas and take measurements. There's no charge for this and absolutely no obligation.

4

You receive a clear, itemised quote

Hope will send over a written quote that breaks everything down — labour, materials, timescale. No vague estimates, no hidden extras. Take as long as you need to look it over.

5

We take care of the rest

Once you're happy and ready to go, we'll book a start date and handle everything from there. Haydn keeps things clean and tidy throughout, and keeps you in the loop at every stage. You just come home to a new bathroom.
