Plumbing Expertise

Reliable Plumbing Services Across Surrey, Kent & Sussex

Good plumbing is the backbone of every well-functioning bathroom, kitchen and utility room. At HGN Bathrooms, plumbing is not an afterthought — it is a core part of what we do. Every renovation we carry out is underpinned by quality plumbing work that is built to last and finished to the highest standard.

Whether you need plumbing as part of a full bathroom renovation or a standalone service such as a radiator installation, tap replacement or washing machine connection, Haydn delivers reliable, clean and professional work across the region.

We pride ourselves on getting the details right first time, using quality materials, and leaving every job site tidy. No shortcuts, no callbacks, no loose ends.

What Plumbing Services Do We Offer?

Our plumbing work covers a wide range of residential tasks, all carried out to a consistently high standard:

  • Full bathroom plumbing — pipework, waste connections, hot and cold feeds
  • Toilet, basin, bath and shower installation and connection
  • Radiator installation, repositioning and replacement
  • Boiler connections and heating system pipework
  • Quooker and boiling water tap installation
  • Washing machine and dishwasher plumbing connections
  • Waste pipe routing and drainage connections
  • Leak repairs and pipework replacement
  • Outside tap installation

Why Choose HGN Bathrooms for Plumbing?

Many homeowners have experienced the frustration of plumbing work that looks acceptable on the surface but is poorly done behind the walls. We take a different approach. Every pipe run is planned for efficiency, properly supported and tested before being closed up. Joints are soldered or crimped correctly, waste connections fall at the right gradient, and nothing is left to chance.

As a one-man operation, Haydn takes personal responsibility for every piece of plumbing work. You will never find untested connections, missing isolation valves or poorly routed pipework on our projects. This attention to detail is what earns us repeat business and word-of-mouth referrals.

Common Plumbing Scenarios

We regularly help homeowners who are upgrading their bathroom and need existing pipework rerouted to accommodate a new layout. We also work with clients who want to add a radiator to a cold room, install a Quooker tap in their kitchen, or connect plumbing for a new utility room.

If you are planning a renovation or simply need a reliable plumber for a standalone job, get in touch for a free, no-obligation quote. We will give you an honest assessment of the work involved and a clear price before we start.

FAQ

Plumbing FAQs

Common questions about our plumbing services.

What plumbing services do you offer?▾
We offer a wide range of plumbing services including bathroom plumbing for renovations, radiator installation and repositioning, boiler connections, Quooker and boiling water tap installations, pipework for washing machines and dishwashers, waste connections and general plumbing repairs.
Can you install a Quooker or boiling water tap?▾
Yes, we regularly install Quooker taps and other boiling water tap systems. This involves fitting the tap unit, connecting the boiler tank beneath the sink and ensuring all water and electrical connections are properly made. We can install these in kitchens, utility rooms or anywhere with suitable access.
Do you handle emergency plumbing repairs?▾
Our main focus is planned plumbing work for bathrooms, kitchens and renovations rather than emergency callouts. However, if you are an existing client or have an urgent issue, please call us and we will do our best to help or recommend a trusted local specialist.
How much does bathroom plumbing cost?▾
Plumbing costs depend on the scope of the work. Simple tasks like fitting a new basin or toilet can start from a few hundred pounds. Full bathroom plumbing as part of a renovation is typically included in the overall project quote. We always provide clear pricing before starting any work.
Are you Gas Safe registered?▾
We handle all general plumbing and water-based work. For gas-specific tasks such as boiler servicing or gas pipe modifications, we work with Gas Safe registered engineers from our trusted network to ensure full compliance and safety.
