Bathroom Renovation FAQs

Common questions from Godstone homeowners.

How much does a bathroom renovation cost in Godstone?▾
Costs vary depending on room size and spec. A typical bathroom renovation in Godstone starts from around £4,000–£6,000 for a standard fit with mid-range tiles and sanitaryware. Luxury renovations with bespoke tiling and high-end fixtures will be higher. We always provide a detailed, itemised free quote before any work begins — no surprises.
How long does a bathroom renovation take?▾
A standard full bathroom renovation typically takes 5–10 working days from strip-out to final finish. Cloakroom refurbs can be done in 2–3 days. We'll give you a clear timeline before we start.
Do you cover Godstone and surrounding areas?▾
Yes — we cover Godstone and all surrounding villages and towns in Surrey. If you're unsure whether we cover your postcode, just give us a call or drop us a message and we'll confirm straight away.
Do you supply the bathroom fittings or do I need to?▾
We're flexible — we can work with fittings you've already purchased, or help you source everything. We have good relationships with suppliers and can often get trade pricing on sanitaryware, tiles and fixtures.

Yes, HGN Bathrooms is fully insured with public liability insurance. We're happy to share details on request. We also have a strong track record on MyBuilder with verified 5-star reviews from homeowners across Sussex, Surrey and Kent.
